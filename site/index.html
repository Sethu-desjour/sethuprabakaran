About me

Hi, I'm Sethu.

I started out writing iOS apps at Mediacorp in 2010. Since then I've co-founded two startups, been CTO at Eatigo, and led engineering at ComfortDelGro, Delivery Hero and Grab.

Over the years I noticed the part of the job I enjoyed most was the 1:1s. Helping someone get through their first few months as a manager. Talking an engineer through whether to take a new job.

People often told me afterwards that it helped to see the problem from a different angle. So I'm doing more of that now.
